# 🎵 Sound Effects Folder

Put your custom sound effects here!

## Supported Formats
- MP3 (.mp3) - Most common
- WAV (.wav) - High quality
- OGG (.ogg) - Good compression

## Sound Files You Can Add

### 1. **jump.mp3**
Plays when the bird jumps/flaps
- Keep it short (0.1 - 0.5 seconds)
- Examples: "whoosh", "boing", "flap", your voice saying "up!"

### 2. **score.mp3**
Plays when you pass a pipe
- Keep it short (0.2 - 0.8 seconds)
- Examples: "ding", "coin", "yay!", your voice cheering

### 3. **game_over.mp3**
Plays when game ends
- Can be longer (0.5 - 2 seconds)
- Examples: "aww", "crash", "explosion", your voice saying "game over!"

## 💡 Tips
- Short sounds work best for games
- Not too loud!
- You can record your own voice!
- Test in the game and adjust

## 🎨 Where to Get Sounds?
- Record your own voice/sounds!
- Search "free game sound effects" online
- Use sites like: Freesound.org, Zapsplat.com
- Make sounds with household objects
- Use your phone's voice recorder

## 🎤 Recording Your Own Sounds (Kids Love This!)
1. Use your phone or computer microphone
2. Make cool sound effects with your mouth
3. Record it
4. Save as MP3
5. Add to this folder!

**Ideas:**
- "Boing!" for jump
- "Ding!" for score
- "Wah wah wahhh" for game over
- Animal sounds
- Silly voices
